  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -w

  CLAIM IT FIRST. The sign-in page offers to create an owner account until
  somebody does, and it asks for this token:

      kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "orchestr8.fullname" . }}-auth \
        -o jsonpath='{.data.ORCHESTR8_BOOTSTRAP_TOKEN}' | base64 -d

    That account owns the organisation. Everyone after joins by invitation.
